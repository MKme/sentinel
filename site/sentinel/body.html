PRIMARY CAPABILITY / PASSIVE WI-FI DETECTION

Detect nearby Wi-Fi activity.
Send the alert over LoRa.

Sentinel listens for qualifying Wi-Fi transmissions and reports activity from your observation point. Devices do not need to join Sentinel's network for detection: the scanner passively receives traffic on the configured Wi-Fi channels, applies your signal-strength threshold, and sends readable alerts to your Meshtastic network.

Deploy it at an equipment site, access route or remote observation point to add a radio-activity cue alongside your other sensors. The XIAO ESP32-S3 performs the Wi-Fi detection; the Wio-SX1262 carries reports over LoRa. No Pi, camera or classifier is required for this core function.

01 / Listen

Scan the configured Wi-Fi channel range with an adjustable dwell time.

02 / Filter

Apply the RSSI threshold and temporary duplicate memory to qualifying transmissions.

03 / Correlate

Optionally combine Wi-Fi activity with an RCWL motion cue within your selected time window.

04 / Alert

Send the node label, detection type, signal strength and Wi-Fi channel over the selected mesh channel.

Tune it for your location.

Use the local boot setup portal to choose the RSSI threshold, Wi-Fi scan range, channel dwell, duplicate-memory interval, alert cooldowns and motion/Wi-Fi combination window. Enable Send detections over Meshtastic and choose the configured output channel. Set the radio region, modem preset and private channel keys through the Meshtastic Bluetooth app.

See what the scanner is doing.

USB diagnostics at 115200 baud show signal strength, Wi-Fi channel, new observations, motion transitions, scanner health, mesh-submission decisions and queue drops. Verbose mode shows each queued qualifying Wi-Fi observation. Confirm reception at another node; submission alone is not delivery.

Activity cues without identity logs.

The current scanner selects randomized or locally administered unicast transmitter addresses and keeps temporary hashes in RAM. Reports contain no MAC addresses, SSIDs or packet contents. Detection is a radio-activity cue, not a person's identity, exact range or a count of people.

Silent devices can remain undetected, and signals can arrive from beyond the immediate mounting location. RSSI is not a distance measurement. The WIFI status counter is cumulative since boot, not a live device population. Size power for continuous reception and use motion sensing while stationary or landed.

Fresh GPS

A usable fix younger than 15 seconds is fresh. Report age includes time waiting in the send queue.

Stale GPS

An older valid fix is marked stale, with its age retained for review.

No GPS

The observation still sends when no usable position is available. XTOC shows NO GPS without inventing a map point.

Coordinates describe the observing sensor, not the detected object's calculated location. Camera images and video stay on the companion or their separate link. Sentinel accepts the documented compact input format, not arbitrary serial text or JSON.

MAVLink / serial ↔ mesh

Connect telemetry
in either direction.

Choose Serial RX to mesh, Mesh to serial TX, or both. Sentinel forwards supported MAVLink telemetry between its separate 3.3 V UART and a configured private Meshtastic channel. USB remains available for the local Meshtastic API and diagnostics.

Supported telemetry

Heartbeat, system status, GPS, attitude and global position. This is a paced, low-rate telemetry bridge; flight commands use the separate paired mode below.

Sentinel map reports

Convert fresh MAVLink positions from the UART or a selected mesh source into T=11 Sentinel packets with a stable node identity and your chosen label.

Familiar hardware

XIAO ESP32-S3 + Wio-SX1262, a suitable antenna and regulated power. UART RX is D7/GPIO44, TX is D6/GPIO43, with common ground and matching baud.

USB ground station / paired LoRa flight link

Plug a ground radio
into your XTOC PC.

The ground radio links XTOC's local Drones Bridge to an aircraft Sentinel connected to the autopilot's MAVLink UART. Supported mission actions travel out; telemetry and acknowledgements return. The flight-controller link uses paired, direct LoRa communication, separate from multi-hop observation reporting.

Observation → rule → review

Make the reports useful
with XTOC triggers.

The optional Sentinel Object Observation Alerts XPASS package provides five SOA trigger templates: fire, smoke, custom object, stale GPS and no GPS. Two local plugin examples cover observation records and location review.

Open XTOC → XPASS, acquire the package, then configure the templates in Triggers. Choose the source, label, confidence and GPS filters; enable the required rule and save. Rules and example plugins start disabled. Use an updated XTOC receiver that understands the observation quality fields.

Sentinel firmware is open source. XPASS templates are optional, separately authorized content. New protected downloads require online authorization; already-authorized installed templates continue working offline.

Practical field roles

Stationary awareness

Monitor motion or nearby radio activity at an equipment site, gate or observation point. Use clear node names and verify reception at the ground station.

SAR & environmental observations

Relay your classifier's visual clues or smoke observations with confidence and position quality so the response team can decide what needs checking.

Airborne reporting

Carry sensing and communications aboard Wraith or another suitable aircraft. Return telemetry and observations while keeping video on its own link.

Training & custom sensors

Build exercise nodes, contact sensors, temperature monitors or battery reports using the standalone examples and documented Sentinel packet format.
